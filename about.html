Who We Are

A Legacy of
Innovation

Founded by the visionary Late Shri Atul J Parekh, Arraycom (India) Limited has been a cornerstone of India's professional electronics landscape since 1992.

Headquartered in the Electronic Zone of GIDC, Gandhinagar, Gujarat, our campus spans approximately 12,035 Sqm with a built-up area of around 80,000 Sqft — purpose-built for precision engineering.

As an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, we maintain rigorous quality standards backed by an in-house R&D center dedicated to advancing next-generation technology.

We stand as a premier System Integrator in India, making significant strides in Satellite Communications, Signal Intelligence, Surveillance, Broadcast, and Networking.
