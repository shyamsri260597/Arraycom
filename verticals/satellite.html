About Satellite Communication Division

Communication & Broadcast Infrastructure

Arraycom’s Satellite Communication Division specializes in designing, engineering, and delivering complete turnkey solutions across Audio-Video Broadcasting, communication, surveillance, intelligence, and networking domains.

With strong technical expertise and decades of experience in professional electronics, we integrate multiple technologies — hardware, software, RF systems, sensors, and communication networks into seamless, reliable, and mission-ready solutions.

Our SI Division is recognized for robust engineering capability, precision execution, best-in-class service support, and the ability to deliver highly customized systems tailored to the requirements of Prasar Bharati (Doordarshan & All India Radio), defence, government, and industrial clients.
