Thick Film Materials

We develop thick film materials using carefully selected precious and base metals, built for strong electrical performance and long-term reliability in electronic applications.

Materials

Metals We Work With

Our material portfolio spans precious metals used for conductivity and stability, alongside base and engineering metals for broader industrial applications.

Precious Metals
Ag
Silver
The primary conductive element in our thick film conductor systems — chosen for its strong electrical conductivity and adhesion.
Pd
Palladium
Combined with silver in our conductor formulations to improve chemical stability and resistance to migration over time.
Pt
Platinum
Used in specialised compositions where thermal and chemical stability matter most, even under demanding conditions.
Ru
Ruthenium
Used as ruthenium oxide in our resistor materials, giving precise and stable resistance characteristics.
Base & Engineering Metals
Al
Aluminium
Powders and flakes used industry-wide as conductive fillers in EMI/RFI shielding composites, gaskets and coatings — valued for their light weight and conductivity.
Cu
Copper
Powders used in conductive pastes and, combined with nickel, in resistor paste formulations as a nitrogen-fireable alternative to noble-metal systems.
Ni
Nickel
Powders used in thick film conductor pastes for multilayer electronic components, and widely used for internal electrodes in multilayer ceramic capacitors.
Ti
Titanium
Used in specialised alloy compositions for resistive thin films, valued for maintaining stable resistivity through repeated heating-cooling cycles.

Application Areas

Industries We Serve

Electronics & Semiconductor Manufacturing

We supply conductive pastes, silver powders and dielectric materials used directly in electronic component manufacturing and hybrid circuit production.

Automotive Electronics

Our silver-based conductive pastes and ruthenium resistor pastes are used in automotive electronics and sensor applications.

Telecommunications

Our hybrid circuit materials and conductive pastes support the circuit terminations and electronic assemblies used in telecom hardware.

Renewable Energy & Solar

Our silver powders and flakes are used for solar cell metallization — a core material input for photovoltaic manufacturing.

Aerospace & Defence

Our platinum-based compositions are built for thermal and chemical stability under demanding operating conditions, suited to aerospace and defence applications.

Industrial Automation

Our resistor pastes and sensor-grade materials support the industrial controls and sensors used in automation systems.
