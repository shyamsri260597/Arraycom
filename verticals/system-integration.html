About the Division

System Integration at Arraycom

Arraycom's Systems Integration Division specializes in designing, engineering, and delivering complete turnkey solutions across Audio-Video Broadcasting, communication, surveillance, intelligence, and networking domains.

With strong technical expertise and decades of experience in professional electronics, we integrate multiple technologies — hardware, software, RF systems, sensors and communication networks — into seamless, reliable, and mission-ready solutions for Prasar Bharati (Doordarshan & All India Radio), defence, government and industrial clients.

Known for robust engineering capability, precision execution, and customized systems tailored to the specific needs of government and defence clients.
