About TFM Division

Our vision is to become the preferred supplier by customizing materials and processes for diverse industrial applications.

Thick Film Materials (TFM) are widely used in hybrid micro circuits, resistors, capacitors, solar cells, and semiconductor packaging.

These materials serve industries such as telecom, automotive, medical, aerospace, defence, and consumer electronics.
