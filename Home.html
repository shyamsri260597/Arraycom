Business Verticals

Six Pillars of Expertise

From satellite uplinks to solar rooftops - our verticals form an integrated ecosystem of precision engineering and technology leadership.

Satellite Communications

End-to-end design, supply and installation of earth station infrastructure - parabolic dish antennas up to 11.3m, VSAT systems, RF chains, HPAs and teleport solutions.

VSAT Earth Stations RF Chains HPAs DSNG

Broadcast & Terrestrial Systems

Supply, installation and commissioning of broadcast infrastructure - UHF/VHF transmitters, DVB-T2 gateways, FM antenna systems, studio integration, signal routing and microwave terrestrial links.

UHF/VHF Transmitters DVB-T2 FM Antennas Studio Integration Microwave Links

Command, Control & Surveillance

End-to-end design and integration of operational control centres - hardware, software, RF systems and communication networks built into reliable, mission-ready solutions for government and defence.

Control Centres CCTV Networks Access Control Perimeter Detection

Solar & Clean Energy

Turnkey solar power projects from site survey to commissioning - grid-tied rooftop, commercial and industrial installations with full O&M support. MNRE & GEDA registered EPC contractor.

Grid-Tied Solar EPC Turnkey O&M Services MNRE & GEDA Registered

Thick Film Materials

Manufacturers of thick film pastes and precious metal powders - solar paste, conductive paste, resistor paste, membrane ink and overglaze paste for hybrid circuits, solar cells and semiconductor packaging.

Solar Paste Conductive Paste Resistor Paste Precious Metal Powders

PCB Manufacturing

In-house printed circuit board manufacturing for professional electronics and industrial applications - part of Arraycom's integrated production capability at the Gandhinagar facility.

Professional Electronics Industrial Applications In-house Production
Who We Are

Thirty Years
of getting it done

Founded in 1992 by Late Shri Atul J. Parekh, Arraycom began as a pioneer in semiconductor materials. Today, we are one of India's leading system integrators delivering mission-critical engineering solutions.

Headquartered in GIDC Gandhinagar, our 80,000 sq ft facility combines expertise across electronics, defence systems, solar technologies, and advanced materials.
